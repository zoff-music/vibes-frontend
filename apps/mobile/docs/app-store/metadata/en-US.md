**Description**

```text
Listen together with Zoff. Create or join a shared music room, add songs from the enabled music providers, and shape the queue together.

With Zoff you can:
• Create a room in seconds or join one by name
• Search enabled music providers and add songs or playlists
• Vote on the shared queue and request the next song
• Keep playback synchronized in server mode
• Let a host control playback in host mode
• Cast a room to a larger screen
• Pair your phone as a remote for another Zoff player
• Choose light, dark, or automatic appearance

Rooms can be public or private, and optional room-admin passwords protect room settings. Provider availability depends on the Zoff service configuration. Music plays through each provider's official embedded player and controls.
```
